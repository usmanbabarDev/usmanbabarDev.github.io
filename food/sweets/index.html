      Is Sweets Halal? What to Check

      Quick answer

      Many chewy sweets are made with gelatine, which in the UK is usually from pork. Sweets set with pectin, starch or agar, or labelled halal or vegan, avoid this.

      Ingredients to watch in sweets & gummies

      GelatineHaramBeef gelatineDoubtfulFish gelatineHalalE440PectinsE406AgarE120Carmine / cochinealE904ShellacE901Beeswax

      Tips

      • "Gelatine" with no source: assume pork.
      • Pectin (E440), agar (E406) and starch are halal gelling agents.
      • Red and pink sweets may use carmine (E120).
