      Is Breakfast cereals Halal? What to Check

      Quick answer

      Most cereals are fine. Check marshmallow pieces (gelatine), flavourings, and emulsifiers. Added vitamin D3 is usually from lanolin (sheep wool), which most scholars accept.

      Ingredients to watch in breakfast cereals

      GelatineHaramE471Mono- and diglycerides of fatty acidsFlavouringsHalalLanolinHalalHoneyHalalE322Lecithins

      Tips

      • Cereals with marshmallows usually contain gelatine.
      • Honey is halal.
