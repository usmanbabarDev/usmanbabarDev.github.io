      Is Energy drinks Halal? What to Check

      Quick answer

      Energy drinks are generally halal by ingredients. Taurine in these drinks is synthetic, not animal-derived. The main question some people raise is the flavouring carrier.

      Ingredients to watch in energy drinks

      FlavouringsHalalE150dSulphite ammonia caramelE160aCarotenes (beta-carotene)E955SucraloseE950Acesulfame KE330Citric acid

      Tips

      • Sweeteners such as sucralose (E955) and acesulfame K (E950) are synthetic.
