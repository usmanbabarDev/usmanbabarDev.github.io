      Is Cakes Halal? What to Check

      Quick answer

      Cakes can contain several things to check: emulsifiers, gelatine in mousse or cheesecake layers, alcohol in soaked or festive cakes, and carmine in red decorations.

      Ingredients to watch in cakes & desserts

      E471Mono- and diglycerides of fatty acidsE475Polyglycerol esters of fatty acidsE477Propylene glycol esters of fatty acidsGelatineHaramSpiritsHaramE120Carmine / cochinealE422Glycerol (glycerine)

      Tips

      • Christmas cakes and puddings often contain brandy or rum.
      • Cheesecakes and mousses often use gelatine.
