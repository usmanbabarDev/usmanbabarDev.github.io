        Tricky ingredients

        A–Z →
        Gelatine✕ Haram

        Treated as haram when the source is not stated, because most gelatine in Europe is from pigs..

        Whey✓ Halal

        Many scholars and certifiers accept whey even from animal-rennet cheese; others avoid it.

        Vanilla extract? Doubtful

        Vanilla extract is made with alcohol.

        Rennet? Doubtful

        Animal rennet is only halal from halal-slaughtered calves.

        Shellfish (prawns, crab, mussels)✓ Halal

        Halal according to the Shafi'i, Maliki and Hanbali schools.

        L-cysteine? Doubtful

        Halal only if fermented or from a halal source..
