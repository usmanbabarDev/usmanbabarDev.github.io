      About these prayer times

      Times are calculated for Rochester (51.388, 0.505) using astronomical formulas. By default we use the Muslim World League method (Fajr 18°, Isha 17°) and the standard Asr, which you can change above — Hanafis should choose Hanafi Asr, which is later. In the UK summer, the sky never gets fully dark, so Fajr and Isha use the widely used "angle-based" rule for high latitudes.

      Your local mosque may use a slightly different timetable, especially for Fajr and Isha in summer and for Jumu'ah (Friday prayer) times. When in doubt, follow your mosque.
